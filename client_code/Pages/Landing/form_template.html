Fair to both sides of the counter.

Most platforms pick a side, and it's usually their advertisers. MonShop makes promises to both of you.

🛍️ To shoppers

  • No ads, ever.
  • Your location is only used to find places near you. We don't store it.
  • Places are sorted by distance and freshness, never by who paid.
  • 5% off at every place you bookmark.

🏪 To shopkeepers

  • Free to list, free to post.
  • Nobody can pay to rank above you.
  • Your photos are yours. Delete them and they're gone.
  • You only give the 5% to people who chose you.
  • New features are voted on by the Shopkeepers' Council.

How the 5% works: a regular's discount, without the paper punch card.

1

Bookmark

Tap 🔖 on any shop or restaurant page. You're now a member there.

2

Show your pass

At the counter, open your pass. It's live: the stripes move and the clock ticks, so a screenshot won't pass.

3

Say the word

Your pass shows today's word, like 🍐 PEAR. The shopkeeper sees the same word on their dashboard. 5% off.
